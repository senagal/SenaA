## Users

The primary users are recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Sena Abdisa Gurmessa for relevant professional opportunities. They need to quickly understand her qualifications, practical experience, technical range, and evidence of achievement, then continue through her CV or contact channels.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Sena's education, skills, projects, credentials, and professional experience in one public profile. It exists to help recruiters and hiring managers assess her fit for data science, AI, and adjacent technical roles and make contact about an opportunity.

Success means a visitor can verify both academic strength and applied ability, review supporting work and credentials, and confidently take the next step through the CV, email, LinkedIn, or GitHub.

## Positioning

Sena combines strong academic performance in computer science with practical AI and data work. The portfolio should substantiate both sides of that position: a 3.94/4.0 summa cum laude record and recognition for academic achievement alongside deployed AI, analytics, dashboard, automation, and software projects.

## Operating Context

The product is a single-page professional portfolio. Visitors move through an introduction, education and skills, completed and upcoming projects, awards and certificates, work experience, and contact options. Supporting evidence is opened through project repositories, a live application, certificate files, the downloadable CV, LinkedIn, GitHub, and email.
