# Design System: Sena Abdisa Gurmessa Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Analytical Editorial"**

This system presents professional evidence with the authority of a carefully edited journal: typographic hierarchy is expressive, facts remain easy to scan, and structure comes from rules and proportion rather than decorative containers. Its mood is confident, credible, and human. Warm paper surfaces keep the experience approachable while dark ink, oxblood, and forest accents lend academic weight.

The interface is spacious without feeling empty. Large EB Garamond headlines establish character; compact Inter labels organize supporting information with analytical precision. The system avoids generic technology gradients, glassmorphism, and dashboard conventions because they would weaken the portfolio's evidence-led editorial voice.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Warm paper grounds paired with near-black ink.
- Editorial serif hierarchy balanced by concise sans-serif metadata.
- Flat, square-edged compositions structured by visible rules.
- Oxblood for decisive action and forest for considered emphasis.
- Generous section rhythm with information-dense rows inside each section.

## Colors

The palette resembles ink and restrained institutional color printed on warm archival stock.

### Primary

- **Oxblood Red:** Reserved for primary actions, dates, statuses, and the smallest decisive accents.
- **Oxblood Deep:** Darkens primary actions on hover without introducing a new hue.

### Secondary

- **Forest Green:** Colors functional accents such as the scrollbar while remaining quieter than oxblood.
- **Forest Deep:** Carries prominent descriptive copy, especially the hero role statement and selected text states.

### Neutral

- **Warm Paper:** The default page ground and navigation surface.
- **Deep Paper:** A recessed background for certificate previews and subtle tonal divisions.
- **Porcelain Surface:** The lightest reading surface and light-on-dark text color.
- **Near-Black Ink:** Primary text, high-contrast surfaces, borders, and the contact panel.
- **Muted Ink:** Secondary explanations, metadata, and supporting copy.
- **Contact Muted:** Secondary text on the near-black contact surface, tinted toward the light foreground rather than neutral gray.
- **Contact Hover:** A softened oxblood tint used only for contact-link hover feedback.
- **Soft Rule / Strong Rule:** Hairline structure for internal grouping and major boundaries.

### Named Rules

**The Rare Oxblood Rule.** Oxblood marks decisions and evidence; it never becomes a broad decorative wash.

**The Paper, Not Glass Rule.** Surfaces are opaque and tactile. Do not use transparency blur, luminous gradients, or glass effects.

## Typography

**Display Font:** EB Garamond (with Georgia and serif fallbacks)  
**Body Font:** Inter (with system sans-serif fallbacks)

**Character:** EB Garamond supplies cultivated editorial confidence while Inter keeps dense project, experience, and metadata content direct. The contrast should feel like a journal headline paired with precise research notes.

### Hierarchy

- **Display** (500, fluid 4–6rem, 0.88 line-height): The full-name hero statement; tightly led and deliberately dominant.
- **Headline** (500, fluid 3–5.5rem, 1.05 line-height): Major section and contact headings, generally capped near 10–14 characters per line.
- **Title** (500, fluid 1.7–2.35rem, 1.05 line-height): Projects, roles, credentials, and other primary records.
- **Supporting Serif Roles** (500, fluid 1.45–3rem): Role statements, featured work, experience, credentials, and subsection headings use content-specific steps within the same family.
- **Statistic** (400, fluid 4.5–7.5rem, 0.8 line-height): Academic evidence and other rare tabular proof points.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, 1.65 line-height): Descriptions and evidence, typically constrained to roughly 52–65 characters per line.
- **Body Large / Small** (400, 1.05rem / 0.9rem, 1.65 line-height): Hero summary emphasis and compact credential explanations.
- **Action** (750, 0.78rem, 0.08em tracking, uppercase): Buttons, text links, and compact interactive labels.
- **Label** (700–800, about 0.68–0.78rem, 0.06–0.11em tracking, uppercase): Navigation, dates, context labels, technology metadata, and actions.

### Named Rules

**The Two-Voice Rule.** Serif communicates identity and subject; sans-serif communicates evidence, navigation, and action.

**The Tight Headline Rule.** Large serif headings use close leading and negative tracking; body copy never inherits that compression.

## Layout

The page uses a centered content frame with a maximum width of 1240px. Main content has fluid horizontal insets, while the fixed navigation spans the viewport and aligns its contents to the same frame. Sections use a generous fluid vertical interval and strong horizontal dividers.

Desktop section introductions use an asymmetric two-column grid: a dominant title opposite a constrained explanation. Project and experience records use multi-column editorial rows rather than cards. At tablet widths these rows collapse to two columns; at mobile widths the layout becomes a single reading column, the navigation becomes a menu, and actions stack when necessary. One-pixel rule gaps and surface contrast organize paired panels such as the academic profile.

**The Outer-Air, Inner-Evidence Rule.** Sections breathe generously; records inside them remain compact enough to compare by scanning.

## Elevation & Depth

The system is flat by default. Depth comes from paper tones, dark/light reversals, borders, and grid seams. A soft ambient shadow appears only on the fixed navigation after scrolling, where it communicates state and separation rather than decoration.

### Shadow Vocabulary

- **Scrolled Navigation** (`0 12px 30px rgba(24, 32, 25, 0.08)`): A subtle state-dependent lift beneath the fixed navigation.

### Named Rules

**The State Earns Shadow Rule.** Resting surfaces stay flat; shadow is reserved for a meaningful change in position or interaction state.

## Shapes

The dominant geometry is square and editorial. Buttons, navigation calls to action, content rows, panels, and certificate frames have no corner rounding. Hairline borders and rectangular divisions provide visual rhythm. The circular scroll-to-top control is a purposeful exception whose silhouette signals a compact floating utility.

**The Square Default Rule.** Use straight edges for content and action surfaces; reserve circles for icon-only floating utilities.

## Components

### Buttons

- **Shape:** Square, compact, and typographically strong with a minimum 48px height.
- **Primary:** Oxblood background, porcelain text, uppercase tracked label, and 0.75rem × 1.25rem padding.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover deepens the oxblood. Keyboard focus uses a 3px oxblood outline with a 4px offset.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Text links use a one-pixel underline and increase their icon gap on hover; navigation CTAs use a dark outline that fills with ink on hover.

### Cards / Containers

- **Corner Style:** Square.
- **Background:** Usually transparent on warm paper; porcelain surfaces appear in paired academic panels, deep paper in media previews, and near-black ink in the contact panel.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Flat at rest; refer to the state-dependent elevation rule.
- **Border:** Strong rules define outer structure; soft rules divide related content.
- **Internal Padding:** Fluid 2–6rem for major panels and roughly 2–3.25rem for repeated records.

### Navigation

The fixed navigation sits on warm paper with a strong bottom rule. The `SG.` serif monogram balances compact uppercase links. Desktop links underline in oxblood on hover; the request-CV action is outlined. On mobile, navigation becomes a full-width vertical list below a three-line menu control, with 48px minimum targets.

### Project and Experience Rows

These signature components treat work history as comparable evidence rather than isolated cards. Thin rules establish rhythm, serif titles lead each row, muted body copy carries detail, and oxblood uppercase labels expose state or context. Current experience rows receive only a subtle paper-tone field, not a promotional badge.

### Contact Panel

The contact area reverses the palette to near-black ink with porcelain type. Its oversized serif invitation leads toward a light primary action and ruled professional-profile links. The reversal marks the portfolio's decisive endpoint without leaving the established palette.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:

- **Do** let typography and ruled structure carry the hierarchy before adding decoration.
- **Do** reserve oxblood for primary action, dates, statuses, and concise emphasis.
- **Do** maintain warm opaque surfaces, visible alignment, and generous section spacing.
- **Do** preserve strong contrast and visible keyboard focus across light and dark surfaces.
- **Do** keep evidence comparable through repeated row structures and tabular number treatment.

### Don't:

- **Don't** introduce generic technology gradients, neon glows, glassmorphism, or dashboard-like cards.
- **Don't** round standard buttons, panels, and content containers.
- **Don't** use shadow as ambient decoration; it must communicate state or elevation.
- **Don't** replace editorial serif hierarchy with an all-sans interface.
- **Don't** dilute factual content with ornamental flourishes or invented visual claims.
